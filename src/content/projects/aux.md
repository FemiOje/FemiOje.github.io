Aux is a shared space for music that does not care which music app you use. People bring their taste, and find new music through each other.

It is built with React, Node, Postgres and Redis. The onchain features on Monad, drop records and tips to curators, are core to it.

Aux started as my idea. We entered it into a hackathon as a team, so the code lives in the team repository, which I forked.

Aux is still in progress.
